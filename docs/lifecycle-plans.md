# Native leaver plans

People shows a leaver preview before a manual termination is approved. The approving administrator is the recorded plan custodian. Bulk termination uses the same exact preview and existing hardware, fresh-email and account-rank checks. A changed inventory requires a new preview.

The inactive person state, original preview, approval digest, custodian, native session and refresh revocation, and the existing supported SCIM and logout queues commit together. Each termination has its own episode; restoring or rehiring the person keeps their identity and cannot revive earlier sessions or refresh grants. Plans add no connector authority or recurring approval.

Leaver plan history on People, and `GET /api/auth/lifecycle/plans?personId=PERSON`, show the original work and deadlines. Reading requires current hardware proof and the existing people-view permission. App scopes hide restricted work and retain an unresolved indicator. Every read rechecks current authority after preparing the projection. Plans are installation-local and excluded from portable archives; person erasure removes their private capture and dependent steps.

SCIM account completion requires a remote inactive or deleted readback. Retries read the remote state before repeating an uncertain destructive request. The existing retry door and maintenance resume the recorded work rather than approving a replacement. Dispatch acceptance and logout acceptance do not prove that every remote session ended. Unsupported sessions, missing accounts, removed connectors and unavailable evidence remain unresolved. A plan never claims full offboarding from these native observations.

The native snapshot records the contracted local-denial and account-verification deadlines and existing escalation interval; it does not qualify timing performance. HR execution, complete joiner and mover plans, published action policies, employer automation integration and authorized vendor qualification remain separate unfinished obligations in CONTRACT.md.
