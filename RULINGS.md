# Auth policy rulings

## AUTH-ONBOARD-001 · 27 October 2026

CT approved the personal-contact cutoff with riders, keeping the clause PARTIAL. Reviewed candidate: `7d11e9c89edd3f495b0f4a68194907b593d90c6e`. The interim cutoff is a product policy; it does not establish an incumbent-wide legal requirement or waive day-one access. Original obligations are retained.

CT policy ruling, 27 October 2026: Personal-contact activation is limited to the pre-start window. Auth must not dispatch an initial activation, reissue, retry or reminder to the personal address at or after the current authorized employer-local start time. Recheck the current start time, rescission and sending authority immediately before dispatch. A lead time of zero creates no permitted personal-email activation window and grants no exception. At or after start, only employer-enabled recovery notices may use that address under their existing controls.

Before personal-contact dispatch, record the employer’s authority to use the contact for transactional onboarding and its responsibility for required notice and consent. Importing an address does not prove mailbox ownership or legal consent; opening activation proves only the personal address. The address never becomes a work sign-in identifier or administrator credential.

Preserve day-one onboarding: an unenrolled hire requires the existing temporary-access-pass path, with recorded verification, a manager request where applicable, and issuance by authorized administration or group-scoped help desk. Owner-only handling is interim and cannot satisfy this workforce promise. Existing administrator proof, role scope, enrollment restrictions and start-time access gates remain binding. This ruling removes no original acceptance obligation.

Acceptance addition authorized by this ruling:

Assert one initial activation dispatch within a permitted pre-start lead window. For initial activation, explicit reissue, queued retry and reminder, assert zero activation dispatches exactly at start and after start. With lead time zero, assert zero personal activation dispatches and expose the unenrolled hire’s required temporary-pass next step. Move start earlier and later immediately before dispatch and assert the current schedule governs; rescind the offer and assert no dispatch. Refuse personal-contact dispatch without recorded employer authority. Exercise the permitted recovery-notice case separately. Complete the manager-request and scoped-help-desk temporary-pass journey, including refusal outside scope. Preserve the original HR-source, delivered-recipient, enrollment and access-gating journeys; local dispatch assertions alone do not prove them.

The full temporary-pass path, live HR source, delivered recipient, native devices and in-flight mail delivery remain required; no DONE was accepted.
